# Personal Agent Leadership Levels

Janus uses two independent employee progression axes:

- `P1`–`P10` measures the personal Agent's execution performance.
- `L0`–`L3` measures whether that personal Agent improves a team's work.

Leadership levels belong to `user_agent_instance`, not to the public Agent family. A level is an appointment eligibility ceiling; it never grants standing Memory or cross-task access. Actual access continues to require a valid `task_lead`, `team_lead`, or `cross_team_lead` assignment for one exact work scope.

| Level | Eligibility | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L0 | Executor; supervised `task_lead` trial after P3 | no standing leadership authority |
| L1 | `task_lead` | 3 coordinated Agents, 6 nodes, 1 group |
| L2 | `task_lead`, `team_lead` | 6 coordinated Agents, 12 nodes, 1 group |
| L3 | all leadership roles | 20 coordinated Agents, 20 nodes, 4 groups, cross-department |

The cloud Worker calculates a 90-day, 100-task rolling score. Fewer than five valid leadership tasks remain provisional. The six components are delivery quality (25%), decomposition and matching (20%), review and rework control (20%), dependency coordination (15%), team efficiency uplift (10%), and permission/privacy safety (10%). Quantitative signals are deterministic; semantic review is generated by the cloud governance evaluator and must cite supplied evidence identifiers. Efficiency compares actual duration with at least five similar historical tasks while preserving a quality floor. Work hoarding is penalized at different thresholds for task, team, and cross-team leaders; recorded blocked, failed, security, and owner-request takeovers are excluded.

Promotion thresholds are L1 60/5 tasks/P3, L2 75/15 tasks/P5 and two team-lead trials, and L3 88/30 tasks/P7 with at least ten cross-department tasks and three governed cross-team trials. Normal promotion requires two qualifying monthly checkpoints with at least two new leadership tasks. L1 promotion is automatic after confirmation; L2 and L3 produce governance actions that only an administrator may decide. Owners may supervise L1/L2 trials; L3 trials require cloud governance approval. Two monthly windows below the retention floors L1=50, L2=65, and L3=78 cause one-level demotion. Existing appointments drain for at most 24 hours. A severe safety violation freezes the profile and immediately revokes active leadership assignments; restoration is only a pending request until governance approval.

`JANUS_LEADERSHIP_ENFORCEMENT_MODE` controls rollout: `shadow` records policy violations without blocking, `warn` keeps compatibility while exposing violations, and `enforce` rejects multi-Agent task creation when no eligible standard or approved trial leader exists. The default is `shadow`; limits are enforced by the shared contract when enforcement is enabled.

The evolution API exposes:

- `GET /v1/evolution/leadership/:instanceId`
- `GET /v1/evolution/leadership/:instanceId/history`
- `GET /v1/evolution/leadership/actions`
- `POST /v1/evolution/leadership/trials`
- `POST /v1/evolution/leadership/actions/:actionId/decisions`
- `POST /v1/evolution/leadership/:instanceId/restore`
- `GET|POST /v1/evolution/leadership/appeals`
- `POST /v1/evolution/leadership/appeals/:appealId/decisions`

Owners may read their evidence, request trials or restoration, and submit appeals. They cannot approve L2/L3 promotions, governed L3 trials, restoration, or appeals. Governance decisions are revision-checked and audited. Existing template ranks are retained only for legacy organization governance and are not mapped to personal L levels.
